When to choose AgentMail

  • Your agent needs to hold conversations: send, reply and forward, not only receive.
  • You want WebSocket events, or custom domains on a $20 plan.
  • You want a larger free message allowance each month.
  • You connect from Claude or ChatGPT and want OAuth sign-in instead of an API key.
  • You want the larger ecosystem: AgentMail is far more widely used today.

When to choose mailsocket

  • The job is getting past a verification step: you want the OTP or magic link as a field, not an email to parse.
  • You want one call that blocks until the code arrives, with no WebSocket client or webhook receiver to run.
  • You write end-to-end tests (pytest, Cypress, Playwright, CI) as well as agents, and want one inbox per test.
