Acceptable use

mailsocket is an inbound-email API for development and testing of apps you own or are authorized to test. You may not use it to send spam, phish, impersonate others, evade another service's rules, facilitate illegal signups, or receive material that is unlawful or infringes another person's rights. Agent and API use must follow the same rules.

Prohibited abuse

You may not resell or expose mailsocket as a disposable-mail service for fraud, account farming, credential abuse, harassment, malware delivery, or circumvention of safety controls. You may not probe, disrupt, overload, or attempt unauthorized access to the service or its users.

Other prohibited uses

You may not create or verify accounts on third-party services you do not own or have permission to test; create accounts in bulk or automatically on third-party services you do not own or have permission to test (account farming); abuse referrals, promotions, or trials; bypass or defeat another service's verification, rate limits, or anti-abuse controls; or resell inbox access.

Enforcement

We may investigate suspected abuse and restrict, suspend, or terminate access without notice when reasonably necessary to protect users, third parties, infrastructure, or the service. We may preserve or disclose information when required by law.

Report abuse

Reports are monitored at abuse@inboxpipe.net. Include the recipient address, relevant timestamps, and enough context to investigate; do not send passwords or unrelated sensitive data.
