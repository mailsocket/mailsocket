Acceptable use

mailsocket is an inbound-email API for legitimate development, testing, and automation. You may not use it to send spam, phish, impersonate others, evade another service's rules, facilitate illegal signups, or receive material that is unlawful or infringes another person's rights.

Prohibited abuse

You may not resell or expose mailsocket as a disposable-mail service for fraud, account farming, credential abuse, harassment, malware delivery, or circumvention of safety controls. You may not probe, disrupt, overload, or attempt unauthorized access to the service or its users.

Enforcement

We may investigate suspected abuse and restrict, suspend, or terminate access without notice when reasonably necessary to protect users, third parties, infrastructure, or the service. We may preserve or disclose information when required by law.

Report abuse

Reports are monitored at abuse@inboxpipe.net. Include the recipient address, relevant timestamps, and enough context to investigate; do not send passwords or unrelated sensitive data.
