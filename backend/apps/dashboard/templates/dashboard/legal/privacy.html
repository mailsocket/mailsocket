Who we are

mailsocket is operated by Bytolab, a sole proprietorship registered in Vietnam, which is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: privacy@mailsocket.app.

What we collect

We store account details, inbox configuration, API and webhook metadata, service logs, and inbound email content needed to provide the service. Inbound data can include envelope and header fields, subject, text and HTML bodies, extracted one-time passcodes, and the original raw message bytes.

When you sign up we record which site or campaign link brought you to the sign-up page (host name and campaign tag only) and store it with your account. We set no tracking or advertising cookies.

Why and how long

We process this data to receive, parse, secure, and deliver email through the API. A scheduled purge permanently deletes committed messages from the live database after the applicable plan window: {{ free_retention_days }} day for Free accounts and {{ paid_retention_days }} days for Pro accounts (longer on higher tiers, as listed on the pricing page). Database backups may retain deleted messages for up to 14 days, and undelivered mail may remain in the mail queue for up to 5 days, before final deletion. Operational and account records may be kept longer where needed for security, billing, legal obligations, or dispute handling.

Location and subprocessors

The service and its PostgreSQL data are hosted on Hetzner infrastructure in Germany, in the European Union. We use these subprocessors:

  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany): servers that run the application, database and mail server.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (global network): DNS, TLS termination and proxying of the website and API through a secure tunnel, forwarding of mail sent to our own contact addresses, and Cloudflare Web Analytics. Web Analytics counts page views in aggregate; it sets no cookies and does not track you across sites.
  • Lemon Squeezy (United States): payments and subscription billing as merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy processes your payment details; we never see your card number.

The same list, with any future changes, is on our Security page.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your account data, subject to legal and security requirements. Email privacy@mailsocket.app. You can also delete your account yourself from Settings in the dashboard.

Security and contact

We use access controls, encryption in transit, and data-minimization measures, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Our website, dashboard, API and webhooks use HTTPS. Inbound email is accepted over SMTP without requiring TLS, so a sender's mail server may deliver a message to us unencrypted. Send privacy questions to privacy@mailsocket.app and abuse reports to abuse@inboxpipe.net.
